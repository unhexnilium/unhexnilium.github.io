A personal schedule manager for Android.

What the app does

YOUR_APP_NAME brings a person's schedule together in one place. It is a personal app, built and maintained by an individual developer.

  • Calendar: a month view that can show events from one or more Google accounts, each with its own colour, together with public holidays and the schedules of favourite baseball teams.
  • To-do: daily checklists in which unfinished items carry over to the next day.
  • Memo: quick notes with pinning, colours and checklists.
  • Habits and subscriptions: habit streaks and a record of recurring payments.

How it uses Google data

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app uses Google Calendar to show your calendars and events and, when you create, edit or delete an event in the app, to apply that change to your Google Calendar. Everything is stored on your own device. Nothing is sent to the developer.

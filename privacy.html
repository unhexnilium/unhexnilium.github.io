Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information YOUR_APP_NAME (“the app”) accesses, how it is used and how it is stored. The app is a personal schedule manager for Android, developed and maintained by an individual developer (“I”, “me”).

Information the app accesses

If you connect a Google account, the app asks for permission to use these Google Calendar scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly: to read the list of calendars in your Google account (their names and colours), so that each calendar can be shown or hidden.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events: to read the events in your calendars (for example titles and dates) and to create, edit or delete events when you do so in the app.

The app also shows the email address and display name of the connected Google account, so that you can tell your accounts apart.

Everything else you enter in the app (to-do items, memos, habits, subscriptions, locally created events and settings) is created by you and stays on your device.

How the information is used

Google user data is used only to provide the app's calendar features that you can see: displaying your calendars and events, and applying your own changes to your Google Calendar. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage, sharing and security

  • Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. I do not operate a server for this app, and I do not receive, collect or have access to your data.
  • The app talks directly from your device to Google's services to read and update your calendar data, and it keeps a copy of your events on your device so that they appear offline.
  • The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to any third party, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • The app's content (events, memos, to-dos, habits and subscriptions) is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup.
  • You can export your data to a file and import it again. The file is saved where you choose (for example on your device or in a cloud drive that you select); the app itself does not send it anywhere.
  • To show public holidays and the schedules of baseball teams you choose, the app downloads public information from Google's public holiday calendars and from public baseball schedule sources. These requests contain no personal data; they name only the country or team you chose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Removing a Google account in the app deletes that account's cached calendar data from your device. It does not delete anything in your Google account.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.
  • You can withdraw the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If the app's use of data changes, I will update this page and the date above.
